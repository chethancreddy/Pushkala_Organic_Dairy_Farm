ABOUT OUR FARM

We Are Farmers Dedicated to 100% Pure Milk

“We are farmers, and we are committed to supplying 100% pure milk without any middlemen or chemicals mixed. Our goal is to reach out to customers directly and provide genuine dairy products.”

At Pushkala Organic Dairy Farm, we believe pure food is the foundation of a healthy life. Located in the serene outskirts of Hulimangala near Jigani, Bangalore, our farm is dedicated to ethical animal husbandry, natural green fodder, stress-free open sheds, and meticulous daily hygiene.

We offer breed-specific transparency — whether you choose A1 HF Cow Milk, indigenous A2 Gir Cow Milk, or rich Murrah Buffalo Milk. Every drop is collected cleanly and delivered farm-fresh to your home.
